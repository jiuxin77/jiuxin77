## Featured project — Milo

**DubHacks '26 · Four-person team project**

Milo is a voice-based shopping prototype for older adults, combining familiar spoken requests with a trusted product catalog and transaction safeguards.

**My role: Risk and Transactions**

- Implemented trusted-catalog checks, risky-product blocking, alternative-product responses, simulated payments, and order recording.
- Contributed to Visa sandbox account validation and the transaction flow; purchases remained simulated.
- Contributed a personal family story to product discussions and helped explore guardian permissions and purchase-record sharing.
- Used AI assistance for testing risk checks, orders, sharing filters, and duplicate requests.
- Prepared product messaging, slides, speaking notes, the demo video script, and judge Q&A.
